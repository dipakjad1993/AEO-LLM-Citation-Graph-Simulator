What Is It?

An enterprise-grade Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) intelligence platform that analyzes how AI models — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek — perceive, rank, and recommend your brand versus competitors. It builds live citation graphs, extracts structured knowledge triples, and maps your brand's semantic positioning across every major LLM.

What Is It For?

If your customers are asking ChatGPT for product recommendations and your brand is invisible, you're losing deals you'll never know about. This tool answers: "What do LLMs actually say about my brand, where do they get it, and how do I fix what's wrong?" — before your competitors figure it out.

What Can It Do?

  • Share of Model Voice (SoMV) — your brand visibility score across every LLM
  • Citation Graph Mapping — see exactly which sources LLMs use and where you're missing
  • Triple Extraction — structured (Subject → Predicate → Object) claims LLMs make about you
  • Sentiment & Bias Detection — find negative patterns and hallucination risks
  • RAG vs Base Weight Attribution — know if your problem is web indexing or pre-training
  • Semantic Embedding Analysis — how LLMs cluster your brand semantically vs competitors
  • Competitive Gap Analysis — exactly where competitors lead and you lag
  • Actionable Recommendations — prioritized fix list with business impact estimates

What Do You Need to Start?

  • Nothing to try it — click "Generate Sample Data" for a full demo with synthetic data
  • API Keys (optional) — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or DeepSeek keys for real LLM queries
  • Your own data (optional) — upload JSON files with prompts, brand entities, or past results
  • Python 3.9+ — for the analysis engine (auto-detected)
  • Node.js 18+ — runs the web dashboard and orchestrator

What to Expect
